About AniLog

Overview

Inspired by MyAnimeList, AniLog is simple and minimalistic. Discover the easiest way to track, manage, and organize your anime watching experience regardless of whether you’re a casual viewer or a hardcore fan. Log your completed shows, keep track of ongoing series, and explore the best the anime world has to offer.
